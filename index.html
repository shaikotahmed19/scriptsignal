Frequently asked questions

How is the AI-style score calculated?

ScriptSignal combines phrasing rules with a local model for word choice, sentence rhythm, and repetition. Select Dialogue / transcript for conversations or spoken transcripts. That setting gives a writing-style score to support editing.

Can the score verify who wrote a script?

Use the score as a starting point for revision, especially for spoken and conversational writing. Establishing authorship requires other evidence, such as the draft history or the writer’s own account.

Where is my script processed?

Your browser calculates the score, generates suggestions, and applies any edits you select. The script stays on your device.
